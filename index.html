Meet the Founder

Owner-led. Local. Built on Hester's name.

Cupboard Warehouse began with Hester — a Lowveld cabinetmaker at heart who was tired of seeing families overpay for kitchens that never quite fit their homes or their budgets. She built Cupboard Warehouse to do it differently: sit down with every client, design around their actual space, and build each piece custom to measure — nothing pre-built, nothing off a shelf.

Hester still sits at the table for every 3-D design, signs off on each quote, and walks the final install before a job is ever called done. When you phone Cupboard Warehouse, you're speaking to the person whose name is on the door — and who answers for the work.
