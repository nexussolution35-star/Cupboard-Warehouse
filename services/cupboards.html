Cupboards made for your home

Every cupboard we build is made to measure for your space — kitchen, bedroom, scullery or storage. You are not squeezing your home into pre-built boxes; the cupboards are built around your walls and your life.

Cupboards for every room

We build custom cupboards for the whole home: kitchen cupboards, built-in cupboards, bedroom cupboards, office cupboards, pantry and laundry cupboards, walk-in closets, wardrobes and TV units. From a full kitchen run to a hallway storage wall, we build cabinetry that uses every centimetre of the space you have — including the awkward corners.

Choose every detail

Door style, colour, finish, handles, internal layout — you choose it all from real samples. We help you balance the look you want against your budget and how the cupboards will actually be used.

Built from certified board

Your carcasses and doors are built from E0 and GREENGUARD Gold certified board — lower-emission materials that are better for your indoor air and built to last in a Lowveld climate.

Soft-close and built to last

Quality soft-close hinges and runners come standard, so doors and drawers close quietly and keep working for years. We install, align and warranty the lot.
