Tops that take real life

A kitchen counter gets hot pots, sharp knives, spills and daily wear. Granite and quartz are built for it — hard-wearing, heat- and scratch-resistant surfaces that still look new years later.

Laser-templated to your cabinetry

Once your cabinets are in, we laser-template the exact shape of your tops — every wall, return and cut-out — so the stone fits your kitchen precisely, with no awkward gaps or filler strips.

Granite or quartz — we help you choose

Granite is natural stone, each slab unique; quartz is engineered for consistent colour and near-zero maintenance. We talk you through the trade-offs and let you pick from real samples.

Seamless joins, clean edges

Joins are placed and colour-matched to be as invisible as possible, and you choose your edge profile. The fabrication is where quality shows, and we do not cut corners on it.

Fitted and sealed

We install the tops level and flush, seal natural stone, and give you simple care guidance so your surfaces stay looking their best.
