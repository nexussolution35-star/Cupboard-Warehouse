Kitchen remodeling, done by the owner

Most cabinet shops run a sales team. Cupboard Warehouse runs an owner. When you walk in, Hester is the person who designs your kitchen, measures it, quotes it and stands behind the build. You deal with the name on the door — from the first sketch to the final handle — not a rotating cast of reps.

Custom-built — nothing pre-made

Every kitchen starts as a blank page. You bring your ideas and choose from real samples — door styles, finishes, colours, handles and stone tops — and we build around your space and your budget. There is no catalogue of pre-built units to squeeze your home into. If something does not fit your wall or the way you cook, we change the design, not your kitchen.

What the design consultation covers

The free consultation is where your kitchen takes shape. We talk through how you cook and live, field-measure every wall, and build a 3-D plan of your real room so you can see the layout, finishes and lighting before a single board is cut. You leave knowing exactly what you are getting and what it costs.

What honest pricing looks like

The quote Hester hands you is itemized — cabinetry, countertops, hardware and installation — line by line, in writing. You see every number. There are no bundled mystery totals that hide the markup, and if anything changes between quote and install, we call you before the crew arrives.

Built to last

Your cabinetry is built from E0 and GREENGUARD Gold certified board, finished and fitted by our own crews, and backed by a workmanship warranty after we leave. We protect your floors and clean up before we hand the kitchen back to you.
