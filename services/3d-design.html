Design before you commit

Before a single board is cut, we model your kitchen or cupboards in 3-D so you can walk through the layout, finishes and lighting. You decide on screen, not on site — and there are no surprises on install day.

We model your real room

This is not a generic template. Hester measures your actual space and builds the design around your walls, windows, plumbing and the way you move through the room, so what you see is what gets built.

Change your mind on screen, not on site

Want the island a little longer, the handles in black, a different door style? We adjust the 3-D plan together until it is exactly right. Iterating on screen costs nothing; changing it after the build is expensive.

What you walk away with

You leave the session with photoreal 3-D visuals, layout options, and an itemized written quote — a clear picture of what you are getting and what it costs, with zero obligation to proceed.

Designed by the owner

The person designing your kitchen is the person who will build it. Hester runs the design herself, so nothing is lost in translation between a salesperson and the workshop.
