About Cupboard Warehouse

Owner on every projectDesign to install

Owner-Led · Local · Mpumalanga

The cabinet warehouse your neighbors recommend

Cupboard Warehouse began as a single cabinet shop with a simple idea: build premium custom kitchens, deal with every homeowner directly, and treat them the way we would want to be treated. Years later we are still owner-led — the person who designs your kitchen is the person who answers the phone.

We design in 3D, build to spec from E0 and GREENGUARD Gold materials, and install with our own crews. Kitchens, islands, vanities and wardrobes, all under one accountable team and one warranty.
